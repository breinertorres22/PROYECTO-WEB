About This Game

Far Cry 6 is a 2021 first-person shooter game developed by Ubisoft Toronto and published by Ubisoft. It is the sixth main installment in the Far Cry series. Set on the fictional Caribbean island of Yara, the game follows Dani Rojas, a local Yaran fighter, as they join a guerrilla revolution to overthrow the dictatorship of Anton Castillo.
