About This Game

Fortnite is the completely free Battle Royale game where you and your friends battle to be the last person standing. Choose from multiple game modes including Battle Royale, Creative, and Save the World. Build structures, craft weapons, and outlast your opponents in this action-packed adventure.

Minimum:

  • CPU: Core i3 2.4GHz
  • RAM: 4GB
  • GPU: Intel HD 4000
  • Storage: 20GB

Recommended:
